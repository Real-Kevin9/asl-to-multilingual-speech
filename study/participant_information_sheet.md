## What is the study about?

We have built a prototype that watches hand signs through a webcam, turns them
into an English sentence, and speaks that sentence aloud in English or Nepali.
We want to find out how easy it is to use, and where it gets in your way. We are
testing the software, not you. There are no right or wrong answers, and nothing
you do can break it.

## What will I be asked to do?

One session of about **20–30 minutes**, in person or over video call:

1. A short demonstration of the system (about 3 minutes).
2. Five short tasks, such as fingerspelling a word and checking that the system
   produced the right letters.
3. A ten-question rating scale about usability, plus a few open questions about
   what worked and what did not.

You can take a break at any point, and you can skip any task or question.

## Who can take part?

Adults (18+) in one of three groups: deaf or hard-of-hearing signers, sign
language interpreters, and hearing people who do not sign. You do not need any
technical background.

## Will I be recorded?

**No video or audio of you is recorded, and no photographs are taken.** The
webcam feed is processed live and is not saved.

The researcher writes down: whether each task was completed, roughly how long it
took, your questionnaire ratings, and any comments you choose to give.

## What data is kept, and who sees it?

You are given a code such as `P04`. Your name is never stored with your answers.
The only record linking your name to your code is the signed consent form, which
is kept separately in a locked location and destroyed after the project is marked.

Your coded answers are stored on an encrypted, password-protected computer **on
the researcher's device only**. They are used to write the dissertation and are
**not shared with other students, published as a dataset, or distributed to third
parties**. Group results (for example, "the average score across eleven
participants was 82") and anonymous quotes may appear in the dissertation. No
quote will include anything that could identify you.

Data will be destroyed by [DATE].

## Do I have to take part?

No. Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time, without giving a reason
and without any consequence. You can also withdraw your data up to **[N] days**
after the session by emailing the researcher with your participant code; after
that the results will have been merged into the analysis and cannot be separated
out.

## Are there any risks or benefits?

There are no anticipated risks beyond the mild frustration of using an unfinished
prototype. There is no payment. Your feedback directly shapes how this kind of
tool is built, and you will receive a short summary of the findings if you want one.

## Limitations you should know about before agreeing

This is a research prototype, not a product. It recognises the manual alphabet
and a limited vocabulary, not full ASL grammar, and it makes mistakes. **It must
not be relied on for anything that matters** — medical, legal, or emergency
communication.
